# Research Brief: ITP Five-Pod Website Strategy

## Decision

The first-wave market pods are:

1. Auto Repair / Body Shops
2. HVAC / Emergency Trades
3. Dental Practices
4. Roofing / Exterior Contractors
5. Med Spas / Aesthetic Clinics

Restaurants move out of the first-wave website build. Veterinary is the best sixth-pod research candidate. Property management has strong workflow depth but weaker entry-wedge clarity and heavier incumbent competition. Legal has attractive economics but should not be approached as one generic vertical.

## Pod Wedges And Expansion Paths

| Pod | Entry wedge | Beyond receptionist | Website proof to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auto Repair | Capture calls while advisors are busy and after hours | vehicle/problem intake, appointment request, estimate follow-up, deferred work, shop owner leak report | live auto demo, operator story, call-to-workflow example |
| HVAC | Capture no-heat/no-cool and peak-season calls | dispatch-ready intake, maintenance agreement renewal, estimate follow-up, technician/owner reporting | emergency call flow, dispatch board concept, after-hours outcome |
| Dental | Recover new-patient calls and reduce front-desk overload | scheduling/confirm queue, recall/reactivation, insurance intake, unscheduled treatment follow-up, production visibility | live dental demo, truthful HIPAA posture, no-clinical-advice boundaries |
| Roofing | Respond instantly during storm and estimate windows | inspection booking, claim intake, estimate/proposal follow-up, production board, job-cost visibility | storm-response flow, lead-speed story, claim-field example |
| Med Spa | Convert high-value consult inquiries before they cool | consult qualification, booking, no-show recovery, reactivation, memberships, treatment/marketing attribution | premium inquiry journey, follow-up sequence, revenue visibility without invented ROI |

## Evidence

- The U.S. repair and maintenance sector had roughly 250,000 private establishments in Q4 2025, supporting a large fragmented service-business base: https://www.bls.gov/iag/tgs/iag811.htm
- The ADA reports persistent staffing pressure, including front-office hiring challenges, and explicitly advises practices to track lost prospective-patient calls: https://adanews.ada.org/ada-news/2025/february/five-years-later-staffing-shortages-infection-control-since-the-covid-19-pandemic/ and https://www.ada.org/resources/practice/practice-management/marketing_inquiriesprospectivepatients
- ServiceTitan's 2025 10-K describes trades businesses as relying on fragmented workflows and needing unified customer, field-workforce, and owner-visibility systems: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000095017025048834/ttan-20250131.htm
- AmSpa reports a $17B+ medical-spa industry growing by more than $1B annually, and describes small med-spa owners personally handling scheduling, inquiries, follow-up, and operations: https://www.americanmedspa.org/med-spa-statistics/ and https://www.americanmedspa.org/news/how-small-medical-spas-can-get-started-with-ai-without-hiring-more-staff/
- The National Restaurant Association reports thin margins: median income before tax of 2.8% for full-service and 4.0% for limited-service restaurants in its 2025 operations data, making restaurants a weaker first-wave buyer despite obvious labor pain: https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/media/press-releases/new-resource-from-national-restaurant-association-provides-insights-into-operational-realities/
- Voice-only reception is increasingly embedded in vertical incumbents, including ServiceTitan, Jobber, Weave, and Dental Intelligence. ITP must sell the operations layer around the call, not the call alone.
