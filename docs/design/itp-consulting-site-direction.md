# ITP Consulting Site Design Direction

This is a constraint packet, not a prescribed mockup. Claude/Fable owns the final art direction after inspecting the current site, strong consulting and vertical-service references, and the available real ITP assets.

## Intended Impression

The site should feel like a small, credible team that learns how a business operates and builds the phone, follow-up, workflow, reporting, and internal tools needed to remove operational drag. It should not feel like a white-label voice bot reseller.

## Visual Rules

- Human and operational before futuristic: real team, real process, real product/workflow evidence.
- Quiet confidence: disciplined typography, useful hierarchy, restrained motion, and visible information architecture.
- Break the current one-note dark brown/orange palette. Keep only brand elements that still serve clarity and credibility.
- Avoid decorative cards, flip-card-only interactions, glow fields, blurred orbs, fake dashboard screenshots, and generic AI imagery.
- Each industry page may have a distinct operational texture, but all pages must feel like one firm.
- Mobile is a first-class sales surface; industry navigation, proof, workflows, and CTAs must remain easy to scan and use.

## Content Rules

- Lead with the owner's operational problem and the business outcome, not a list of AI features.
- Show the progression: answer and capture -> qualify and route -> follow up and book -> operate and report -> build custom internal tools.
- Use only real founders, operators, podcasts, demos, customer proof, and metrics that can be verified.
- Use AI-generated imagery only as supporting editorial art, never as implied customers, employees, or testimonials.
- Public v1 pricing defaults to "book a call" until packages and proof are standardized.

## Required Proof

Fresh screenshots at desktop and mobile widths for the homepage and each industry page, plus a visible comparison against this direction and a list of every remaining compromise.
